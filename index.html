E-commerce

Liquor e-commerce: ≈30× ROAS from a lean Google Ads budget

An online liquor store that needed more sales from Google without a large budget. Paid search was built around purchases and revenue, and SEO ran alongside as a separate, longer-term channel.

Strategy

Strategy

How I plan each channel

The working order I follow for SEO, Google Ads and Meta Ads. Tracking comes before spend, and every channel is measured against a business number.

SEO

Visibility that compounds over time.

  1. Technical foundationCrawling, indexing and site speed
  2. Keyword and intent mappingWhich page answers which search
  3. Page optimizationContent, service and product pages
  4. Structured dataSchema / JSON-LD for rich results
  5. AEO / GEOVisibility in AI-generated answers
  6. MeasureSearch Console and GA4

Google Ads

Capture demand and spend toward revenue.

  1. Conversion trackingGTM, GA4 and Google Ads conversions
  2. Structure by intentSeparate product, brand and generic searches
  3. Product feedMerchant Center for Shopping and PMax
  4. Campaign mixSearch, Shopping and Performance Max
  5. Bid toward valueOptimize for conversions and revenue
  6. ReviewROAS, search terms and budget shifts

Meta Ads

Create demand and turn it into leads.

  1. Audience and offerWho to reach and what to say
  2. AwarenessBuild familiarity with the brand or project
  3. Lead generationCampaigns built to collect enquiries
  4. Cost per leadTrack what each lead costs
  5. Lead qualityCheck which leads turn into real conversations
  6. ReallocateMove budget to what performs
